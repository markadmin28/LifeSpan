# Privacy Policy for LifeSpan

**Effective date:** August 9, 2026  
**App name:** LifeSpan  
**Package name:** `com.lifespanbattery.app`  
**Developer:** markadmin28  
**Contact:** [Open a GitHub issue](https://github.com/markadmin28/LifeSpan/issues)

This Privacy Policy describes how LifeSpan (“the App”) handles information when you use the Android application published on Google Play.

---

## 1. Summary

LifeSpan is a **battery health and charging monitor**. Core battery readings, charge sessions, temperature logs, theme settings, and preferences are stored **on your device**.

The App does **not**:
- require an account or login
- sync your battery history to our own servers
- sell your personal information
- include a first-party analytics SDK for tracking you

The App **may** use third-party Google services (AdMob and Google Play Billing) when you choose ads or purchases. Those services process limited data under Google’s own policies.

---

## 2. Information We Process

### 2.1 Information stored on your device

Processed locally on your phone (and optionally Wear OS companion features) to provide LifeSpan’s features:

- Battery level, temperature, charging status, power estimates, and related system battery signals
- Charge session history and telemetry logs you generate while monitoring
- Alert thresholds and app settings (themes, bubble preferences, reliability options, etc.)
- Cosmetic unlock / reward progress and purchase entitlements stored in local preferences
- Optional high-usage app rankings derived from Android usage stats (labels/package names shown in-app)

This data remains on your device unless you back up or transfer the device using Android system backup tools.

### 2.2 Information processed by third parties (optional features)

| Feature | Provider | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Rewarded ads (unlock themes) | Google AdMob | Serve and measure ads when you choose to watch them |
| In-app purchases & subscriptions | Google Play Billing | Process Theme Studio purchases |
| Network connectivity | Google Play services / device network | Required for ads and billing |

When these features run, Google may collect device and advertising identifiers, IP address, coarse device/app information, and purchase records according to Google’s policies. LifeSpan does not operate a separate cloud database of your battery history.

---

## 3. Permissions and Why We Ask

| Permission / access | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Internet / network state | Ads, Play Billing, and related Google services |
| Notifications | Status and alert notifications you enable |
| Foreground service | Continuous battery / thermal monitoring while enabled |
| Display over other apps (overlay) | Optional floating charging bubble |
| Usage access | Optional high-usage app insights (local only) |
| Ignore battery optimizations | Optional more reliable background monitoring |
| Boot completed | Optional auto-start of monitoring after reboot |
| Vibration | Alert feedback |

You can deny or revoke optional permissions in Android settings. Core monitoring features that depend on a permission will be limited if that permission is unavailable.

---

## 4. How We Use Information

We use on-device information to:

- Show live battery, temperature, and charging status
- Detect charge-limit / overheat / rapid-rise conditions and alert you
- Power widgets, Quick Settings tile, and Wear companion surfaces
- Remember your settings and Theme Studio unlocks
- Improve reliability of monitoring you turn on

We use third-party Google services only to:

- Show rewarded ads when you request an unlock via ads
- Complete purchases/subscriptions you initiate

We do **not** sell personal information.

Data may be processed by:

- **Google Play / Google LLC** — distribution, billing, ads, and Play services  
  - [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

## 6. Data Retention and Deletion

- Local LifeSpan data remains on your device until you clear app storage, uninstall the App, or delete related data in system settings.
- Purchase records and ad-related records retained by Google follow Google’s retention practices.
- Uninstalling LifeSpan removes the App’s local database and preferences from the device (subject to Android backup restore behavior).

## 7. Children’s Privacy

LifeSpan is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through Google services used by the App, contact us and we will help address it as appropriate with Google’s tools and policies.
