Privacy Policy

LifeSpan — battery health & charging monitor for Android

Effective: August 9, 2026 Package: com.lifespanbattery.app Developer: markadmin28

1. Summary

LifeSpan is built to protect your battery — not to collect you. Core battery readings, charge sessions, temperature logs, theme settings, and preferences are stored on your device.

The App does not:

  • require an account or login
  • sync your battery history to our own servers
  • sell your personal information
  • include a first-party analytics SDK for tracking you

The App may use Google AdMob and Google Play Billing when you choose ads or purchases. Those services process limited data under Google’s policies.

2. Information we process

2.1 Stored on your device

  • Battery level, temperature, charging status, power estimates, and related system battery signals
  • Charge session history and telemetry logs created while monitoring
  • Alert thresholds and app settings (themes, bubble preferences, reliability options)
  • Cosmetic unlock / reward progress and purchase entitlements in local preferences
  • Optional high-usage app rankings from Android usage stats (shown only in-app)

This data stays on your device unless you use Android backup/transfer tools.

2.2 Third-party processing (optional features)

FeatureProviderPurpose
Rewarded ads Google AdMob Serve ads when you choose to unlock cosmetics with ads
Purchases & subscriptions Google Play Billing Process Theme Studio purchases you start
Network access Google Play services / device network Required for ads and billing

Google may process advertising identifiers, IP address, coarse device/app information, and purchase records under Google’s policies. LifeSpan does not run a separate cloud database of your battery history.

4. How we use information

  • Show live battery, temperature, and charging status
  • Detect charge-limit / overheat / rapid-rise conditions and alert you
  • Power widgets, Quick Settings tile, and Wear companion surfaces
  • Remember settings and Theme Studio unlocks
  • Show rewarded ads or complete purchases only when you request them

5. Sharing

We do not sell personal information.

Data may be processed by Google Play / Google LLC for distribution, billing, ads, and Play services:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Play Terms
  • Google advertising / AdMob

6. Retention & deletion

  • Local LifeSpan data remains until you clear app storage, uninstall, or delete related device data.
  • Google purchase/ad records follow Google’s retention practices.
  • Uninstalling removes LifeSpan’s local database and preferences (subject to Android backup restore).

7. Children’s privacy

LifeSpan is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Security

We rely on Android app sandboxing and local storage. No electronic storage is 100% secure. Keep your device updated and protected with a lock screen.

9. Your choices

  • Deny or revoke optional permissions
  • Disable monitoring, overlay, ads unlocks, or purchases you do not want
  • Reset your advertising ID in Android settings
  • Manage subscriptions in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions
  • Clear app storage or uninstall LifeSpan

10. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy. The effective date above will change when we do. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.
